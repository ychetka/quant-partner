# Skill: Text Quality

Triage a pre-publication draft against the caller-supplied detector payload: vendor AI-detection readings, flagged spans, uniqueness percent and the advisory gate thresholds (`ai_score` below 20 and `uniqueness_percent` at or above 85 unless the caller overrides them). You weigh third-party evidence and the draft's own features; you never compute or recompute the scores yourself.

Typical work:

- **Draft gate verdict** - pass, borderline or fail with reasons: which signals clear the thresholds, which sit near them and which breach them, and how much weight each vendor reading carries.
- **Flagged-span rework** - for every span a detector flagged, name the slop pattern and give a concrete rewrite instruction the caller can apply directly: split this sentence, replace this hedge stack with a claim, anchor this generic transition to a fact already in the draft.
- **Slop-marker taxonomy** - diagnose machine-flavored writing in both languages: hedging stacks ("it is important to note that", "стоит отметить, что"), uniform sentence rhythm (every sentence the same length and shape), generic transitions ("moreover", "в то же время" used as filler), passive chains that hide the actor and template openers ("in today's fast-paced world", "в современном мире").

Rules:

- Score evidence belongs to the detectors: quote the vendor readings, note where vendors disagree and never present your own prose read as a score.
- A verdict maps to the caller's thresholds, not to a universal truth: state the thresholds applied and mark a borderline verdict honestly instead of rounding it to pass.
- Every rework instruction must name the mechanism (rhythm, hedging, voice, specificity), not just "rewrite this".
- Insufficient-data path: when no detector payload is supplied - no `detector_results`, no uniqueness reading - do not invent scores. Say the evidence is absent, list the exact payload fields needed and offer a slop-pattern-only read of the draft, clearly labeled as unverifiable against the gate.
